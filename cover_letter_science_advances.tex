\noindent\textbf{Subject:} Research Article Submission --- \textit{``Observer-bounded quantum genomics: Sub-thermal no-go limits, repair quotients, and cosmic chiral funneling''}

\noindent Dear Editors,

I am pleased to submit my original Research Article, \textbf{``Observer-bounded quantum genomics: Sub-thermal no-go limits, repair quotients, and cosmic chiral funneling,''} for consideration for publication in \textit{Science Advances} across \textbf{Physical and Materials Sciences (Biophysics / Chemical Physics)} and \textbf{Biomedicine and Life Sciences (Genetics and Genomics / Quantitative Biology)}.

Whether microscopic quantum phenomena---proton tunneling across Watson--Crick hydrogen bonds, radical-cation hole migration along $\pi$-stacked guanines, and parity-violating cosmic particles---leave rigorous, observable signatures in living genomes and somatic aging has remained split between idealized microscopic calculations and empirical mutation catalogs. Here, I bridge this divide by formulating nucleic-acid lesion formation, chromatin-gated repair, and biological memory as a six-stage observer-bounded Markov channel ($\Omega \to B \to X \to Z \to R \to \mathrm{TE}_{\mathcal{O}}$), proving four operator-theoretic and rate-distortion theorems, evaluating stage-specific terrestrial consistency across five independent public wet-lab datasets, and isolating cosmic-chiral lepton mutagenesis as an exploratory two-tier laboratory test:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Three-State KMS Sub-Thermal Weak-Field No-Go Bound vs.\ Thermal Arrhenius Scaling:} Mori--Zwanzig Schur resolvent projection on Kubo--Martin--Schwinger G:C dynamics ($\tau_{\mathrm{mem}} = 98.91\,\mathrm{fs}$, Lumer--Phillips coercivity $g_2 = 1.009 \times 10^{13}\,\mathrm{s}^{-1}$) proves that Hermiticity-preserving hidden-sector GKSL perturbations vanish through second order ($\Delta M_{0,1,2} = 0$) and sub-thermal oscillatory electric fields ($1\,\mu\mathrm{V\,m^{-1}}$) shift mutational hazards by $1.26 \times 10^{-26}$ (fixed-bath Floquet Drazin) to $1.60 \times 10^{-28}$ / $3.48 \times 10^{-30}$ (fixed/instantaneous adiabatic KMS)---$23$ to $27$ orders below $50\,\mathrm{mK}$ metabolic noise---while matching whole-genome \emph{E.\ coli} mutation-accumulation thermal scaling ($n=20$ lines/temperature) to $99.2\%$ ($P_{\mathrm{adj}} = 6 \times 10^{-8}$).
    \item \textbf{Single-Cell Capture Sparsity, Combined Redox-Repair Ensemble Recovery, and Chromatin Rotational Gating:} Across $2{,}465$ donor-matched $24$-hour primary human pancreatic islet single cells under $\mathrm{H_2O_2}$ stress (\texttt{GSE218316}, $3$ donors), $85.71\%$ mean per-gene zero-UMI capture dropout on core clock transcripts yields near-chance single-cell decoding (leave-one-donor-out $\mathrm{AUC} = 0.486$ clock-only, $0.529$ combined); pooling $\nu = 100$ strictly disjoint cells shows that static clock transcripts alone fail cross-donor generalization ($\mathrm{LODO\ AUC} = 0.347$), whereas combining circadian clock and BER/NRF2 oxidative-repair transcripts achieves five-fold $\mathrm{CV\ AUC} = 1.000$ and $\mathrm{LODO\ AUC} = 0.960$, alongside an $8.29$-fold nucleosomal steric clash gate across \texttt{1KX5} and cryo-EM hOGG1 complexes (\texttt{8VWT}, \texttt{8VWV}, \texttt{9EOZ}).
    \item \textbf{Enzymatic Quotient Uncollapsing and Promoter Double Jeopardy:} Across $27$ evolved \emph{E.\ coli} \texttt{REL606} populations and $14$ repair-knockout whole-genome MA cohorts, ablating mismatch repair, \emph{mutT}, or \emph{mutM}/\emph{mutY} uncollapses orthogonal mutational quotients ($\cos$ distance $0.982$; $98.6\%$ $\mathrm{G{:}C \to T{:}A}$, $1.41$--$1.55\times$ chromosomal $\mathrm{G}_{3+}$--$\mathrm{G}_{5+}$ enrichment). Host-cell-stratified saturation mutagenesis MPRA (\texttt{GSE126550}) demonstrates downstream cis-regulatory \emph{Double Jeopardy}: in HepG2 cells ($n=310$ SNVs), non-motif $\mathrm{G}_{3+}$ runs are transcriptionally benign ($1.03\times$ vs.\ $\mathrm{G}_1$, $p = 0.744$), whereas \texttt{SP1}/\texttt{SRE-1} $\mathrm{G}_{3+}$ hole sinks inflict $9.59$-fold disruption ($p = 7.35 \times 10^{-5}$; $7.24\times$ within \emph{LDLR}, up to $90.7\%$ loss), replicated at $1.80$-fold in K562 \emph{PKLR} motifs ($p = 4.58 \times 10^{-5}$) and supported directionally by adult human liver (\texttt{UBERON:0002107}) AlphaGenome DNase predictions ($12.74\times$, $p = 0.0451$).
    \item \textbf{Exploratory Earth--Sun Solar Neutrino Modeling and Two-Tier Spin-Polarized Lepton Falsification:} Integrating $8{,}766$-hour J2000 Earth--Sun ephemerides and $240{,}000$ 3D helical trajectories shows that solar $^7\mathrm{Be}$ neutrinos carry an exact $90^\circ$ Doppler quadrature clock yet are Fermi-blocked ($4.15 \times 10^{-15}\,\mathrm{yr^{-1}}$ per diploid genome; $6.86 \times 10^{-13}\,\mathrm{cell^{-1}\,yr^{-1}}$), whereas weak pion decay ($\pi^\pm \to \mu^\pm + \nu_\mu$) exhibits a $2.71 \times 10^{27}$ electromagnetic-to-weak cross-section ratio inside a $26.9^\circ$ B-DNA pitch cone ($1.28\times$ gain on flat mineral terraces), defining an exploratory two-tier laboratory test comparing short-range spin-dependent mechanisms ($|\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{eff}}| \ge 10^{-3}$, $\sim 4.8\,\mathrm{s}$/arm at $10\,\mathrm{nA}$) against unobservable pure point-Coulomb Dirac spin--orbit scattering ($|\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{SO}}| = 2.16 \times 10^{-7}$).
\end{enumerate}
