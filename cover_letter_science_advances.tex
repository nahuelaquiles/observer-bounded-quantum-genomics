\noindent\textbf{Subject:} Research Article Submission --- \textit{``Observer-bounded quantum genomics: Sub-thermal no-go limits, repair quotients, and cosmic chiral funneling''}

\noindent Dear Editors,

I am pleased to submit my original Research Article, \textbf{``Observer-bounded quantum genomics: Sub-thermal no-go limits, repair quotients, and cosmic chiral funneling,''} for consideration for publication in \textit{Science Advances} across \textbf{Physical and Materials Sciences (Biophysics / Chemical Physics)} and \textbf{Biomedicine and Life Sciences (Genetics and Genomics / Quantitative Biology)}.

Whether microscopic quantum phenomena---proton tunneling across Watson--Crick hydrogen bonds, radical-cation hole migration along $\pi$-stacked guanines, and parity-violating cosmic particles---leave rigorous, observable signatures in living genomes and somatic aging has remained split between idealized microscopic calculations and empirical mutation catalogs. Here, I bridge this divide by formulating nucleic-acid lesion formation, chromatin-gated repair, and biological memory as a six-stage observer-bounded Markov channel ($\Omega \to B \to X \to Z \to R \to \mathrm{TE}_{\mathcal{O}}$), proving four exact operator-theoretic and rate-distortion theorems and evaluating stage-specific empirical consistency across five independent public wet-lab datasets:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Sub-Thermal Weak-Field No-Go Bound vs.\ Thermal Arrhenius Scaling:} Mori--Zwanzig Schur resolvent projection on Kubo--Martin--Schwinger G:C dynamics ($\tau_{\mathrm{mem}} = 98.91\,\mathrm{fs}$, Lumer--Phillips coercivity $g_2 = 1.009 \times 10^{13}\,\mathrm{s}^{-1}$) proves that hidden-sector perturbations vanish through second order ($\Delta M_{0,1,2} = 0$) and sub-thermal oscillatory electric fields ($1\,\mu\mathrm{V\,m^{-1}}$) shift mutational hazards by $1.26 \times 10^{-26}$ (fixed-bath Floquet Drazin) to $3.48 \times 10^{-30}$ (adiabatic KMS)---$23$ to $27$ orders below $50\,\mathrm{mK}$ metabolic noise---while matching whole-genome \emph{E.\ coli} mutation-accumulation thermal scaling ($n=20$ lines/temperature) to $99.2\%$ ($P_{\mathrm{adj}} = 6 \times 10^{-8}$).
    \item \textbf{Transcriptomic Capture Sparsity vs.\ Tissue-Ensemble Fidelity and Chromatin Rotational Gating:} In $4{,}298$ primary human pancreatic islet single cells under $\mathrm{H_2O_2}$ stress (\texttt{GSE218316}), $99.57\%$ zero-UMI droplet capture dropout on detected clock transcripts collapses single-cell decoding ($\mathrm{AUC} = 0.502$), whereas $1{,}000$-cell ensembles recover $\mathrm{AUC} = 0.936$ ($\mathrm{LODO} = 0.901$) consistent with non-asymptotic van Trees--Clarke--Barron scaling, while nucleosomal superhelical phase imposes an $8.29$-fold steric gate ($p = 1.18 \times 10^{-32}$) across $266$ base-pair steps of \texttt{1KX5} and cryo-EM hOGG1 complexes (\texttt{8VWT}, \texttt{8VWV}, \texttt{9EOZ}).
    \item \textbf{Enzymatic Quotient Uncollapsing and Promoter Double Jeopardy:} Across $27$ evolved \emph{E.\ coli} \texttt{REL606} populations and $14$ repair-knockout whole-genome MA cohorts, ablating mismatch repair, \emph{mutT}, or \emph{mutM}/\emph{mutY} uncollapses orthogonal mutational quotients ($\cos$ distance $0.982$; $98.6\%$ $\mathrm{G{:}C \to T{:}A}$, $1.41$--$1.55\times$ chromosomal $\mathrm{G}_{3+}$--$\mathrm{G}_{5+}$ enrichment). Cell-line-stratified saturation mutagenesis MPRA (\texttt{GSE126550}) demonstrates \emph{Double Jeopardy}: in HepG2 cells ($n=310$ SNVs), non-motif $\mathrm{G}_{3+}$ runs are transcriptionally benign ($1.03\times$ vs.\ $\mathrm{G}_1$, $p = 0.744$), whereas \texttt{SP1}/\texttt{SRE-1} $\mathrm{G}_{3+}$ hole sinks inflict $9.59$-fold disruption ($p = 7.35 \times 10^{-5}$; $7.24\times$ within \emph{LDLR}, up to $90.7\%$ loss), replicated at $1.80$-fold in K562 \emph{PKLR} motifs ($p = 4.58 \times 10^{-5}$).
    \item \textbf{Earth--Sun Solar Neutrinos vs.\ Two-Tier Spin-Polarized Lepton Falsification:} Integrating $8{,}766$-hour J2000 Earth--Sun ephemerides and $240{,}000$ 3D helical trajectories shows that solar $^7\mathrm{Be}$ neutrinos carry an exact $90^\circ$ Doppler quadrature clock yet are Fermi-blocked ($4.15 \times 10^{-15}\,\mathrm{cell^{-1}\,yr^{-1}}$), whereas weak pion decay ($\pi^\pm \to \mu^\pm + \nu_\mu$) amplifies chiral ionization $2.71 \times 10^{27}$-fold inside a $26.9^\circ$ B-DNA pitch cone ($1.28\times$ gain on flat mineral terraces), defining an open two-tier laboratory test that separates resonant CISS/DEA spin-exchange ($|\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{eff}}| \ge 10^{-3}$, $\sim 4.8\,\mathrm{s}$/arm at $10\,\mathrm{nA}$) from unobservable pure point-Coulomb Dirac spin--orbit scattering ($|\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{SO}}| = 2.16 \times 10^{-7}$).
\end{enumerate}
